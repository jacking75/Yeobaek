# English Store listing

The reserved product name is `여백` in all four Store listings. The English description uses its romanized name, `Yeobaek`.

## Description

Yeobaek is a free Windows browser focused on reading blogs and technical articles.

Reduce known ad requests, select distracting elements to hide, and read article text in a spacious reader view. Keep links in a reading queue and save articles and images on your PC to read again.

Features include:

- Known ad request blocking and per-site exceptions
- Element picking with saved rules for each site
- Reader mode with adjustable font, spacing, width and theme
- Tabs, separate windows and link context menus
- A reading queue
- Local article archives and saved text search
- Korean, English, Japanese and Simplified Chinese interfaces

Saved articles and settings stay on your PC. Yeobaek does not sync them to a developer server. Browsing and searching require internet access. Images that could not be downloaded and other external content may be unavailable offline. Ad blocking and article extraction depend on the site's structure.

The app is free, with no in-app purchases or paid trial.
